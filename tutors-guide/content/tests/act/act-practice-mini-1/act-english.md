### The Foundry of Pietrasanta

Nestled in northern Tuscany, the marble studios of Pietrasanta have hosted artists since the Renaissance. **[1] Having arrived at the studio before sunrise the sculptors** prepared their chisels and pneumatic hammers for the demanding workday. 

The town's craftsmen gather **[2] on an annual recurring basis each year** to display experimental bronzes and marble bas-reliefs in the central piazza, sustaining a historic artisanal tradition.
